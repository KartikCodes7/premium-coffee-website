A premium Authentication and Onboarding page for RestaurantOS. Managers and staff use this to securely access the platform.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Colors: Canvas Charcoal (#0B0C0E) main canvas, Deep Surface (#12141C) cards, Glass Card Fill (rgba(25, 28, 41, 0.45)), Lux Gold (#E5C158) accent, Premium White (#F4F5F6) headers, Muted Steel (#8E939E) secondary text.
- Glassmorphic panels with Ice Border (1px border, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)) and subtle backdrop-blur.
- Premium typography: Bold track-tight sans-serif display headers, clean mono for numbers, relaxed leading for body copy.
- Ambient visual background (dark, abstract luxury, blurred).

**Page Structure:**
1. **Background:** A stunning full-viewport background with deep dark tones and subtle Lux Gold ambient glows, conveying a Michelin-star dining atmosphere blurred out in the background.
2. **Central Container:** A perfectly centered, sleek, glassmorphic sign-in card with an Ice Border.
3. **Card Content:**
   - The "RestaurantOS" logo and a brief subtitle: "Intelligence for the Modern Table."
   - A modern form layout: Email input and Password input. Inputs should have transparent backgrounds with bottom borders that illuminate Lux Gold on focus.
   - A "Remember me" toggle switch (sleek pill design) and "Forgot Password?" link.
   - A primary "Access Operating System" button in Lux Gold with a subtle glow and hover lift.
   - Alternative sign-in options: "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple" using subtle outline buttons with SVG icons.
4. **Footer:** Small text at the bottom of the card: "Secure encrypted connection. View Privacy Policy."
